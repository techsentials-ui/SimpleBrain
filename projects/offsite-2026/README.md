# Offsite 2026 — "How AI Rebuilt Our Back Office"

**Event:** TechSentials off-site business weekend (first one), weekend of 2026-10-10.
**Audience:** Jeff (COO) + leadership.
**Goal:** Show what AI integration delivered in 2026 — money saved, capacity gained, real delivery work — then set direction for 2027.
**Status:** Planning. Outline drafted; numbers not yet collected.
**Source:** [raw brain dump](../../raw/2026-10-08-offsite-ai-story-braindump.md)

## The story in one line

We lost two big contracts and our paid BD spend didn't convert — so we stopped buying services and built them ourselves with Claude. A small back office now runs like a multi-person shop, at a fraction of the cost, and we're submitting more than ever.

## Narrative arc

1. **Where we were** — honest reset on 2026.
2. **The pivot** — insource instead of procure.
3. **What we built** — BD engine + delivery engine (the showcase).
4. **What it got us** — numbers: dollars saved, submissions made, hours returned.
5. **Honest scoreboard** — no wins yet; submissions are leading indicators.
6. **New door: ABTX** — partnership + D.C. lobbyist access to contracting offices.
7. **2027** — focus, opportunities, tools, capabilities, partners.
8. **Decisions for this weekend.**

Lead with the honesty (losses, no wins yet). It makes the efficiency claims credible to a COO.
